The Problem

Patients with complex, rare, or high-stakes medical conditions face significant challenges in finding the right specialist.

❌ Conflicting Opinions

Multiple doctors provide different recommendations, leaving patients confused about the best treatment path.

❌ No Reliable Expert Identification

Difficult to distinguish true experts from those with inflated credentials or popularity-based rankings.

❌ Pre-Surgery Verification

Patients want to verify their surgeon's qualifications before committing to major surgical procedures.

❌ Limited Information on Options

International patients especially struggle to navigate the US healthcare system and understand available options.

❌ Biased & Incomplete Solutions

Current solutions (referrals, internet searches, AI tools) are often biased, incomplete, or pay-to-play.
